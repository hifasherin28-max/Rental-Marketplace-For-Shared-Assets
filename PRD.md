# Product Requirements Document (PRD)

 ## Rental Marketplace for Shared Assets

 ### 1\. Product Overview

 A digital marketplace that allows users to list, discover, rent, and manage shared assets such as tools, vehicles, equipment, electronics, and other rentable items.

 ### 2\. Problem Statement

 Many assets remain unused for long periods while others need them only occasionally. The platform helps owners generate income from unused assets while giving renters affordable access without purchasing them.

 ### 3\. Target Users

 - **Asset Owners:** Individuals or businesses who want to rent out unused assets.
- **Renters:** Users looking for affordable, short-term access to assets.
- **Administrators:** Platform managers responsible for users, listings, payments, and disputes.

 ### 4\. Core Features

 - User registration and login
- Asset listing and management
- Search and category-based browsing
- Filters for price, location, and availability
- Booking and rental management
- Online payments
- Reviews and ratings
- Notifications

 ### 5\. Asset Listing

 Owners should be able to add assets with photos, descriptions, pricing, location, availability, rental rules, and security-deposit information. Owners can also edit, pause, or remove their listings.

 ### 6\. Search & Discovery

 Renters can search for assets using keywords and categories. The platform should provide filters such as location, price range, availability, asset type, and ratings.

 ### 7\. Booking & Rental Process

 Renters can select an available asset, choose rental dates, submit a booking request, and complete payment. Owners can accept or reject requests, while both parties can track rental status.

 ### 8\. Payments & Security

 The platform should support secure online payments, rental fees, security deposits, refunds, and transaction records. Payment status should be visible to both owners and renters.

 ### 9\. Trust, Safety & Reviews

 Users should have verified profiles and the ability to rate and review completed rentals. The platform should provide reporting, dispute handling, cancellation policies, and mechanisms to reduce fraudulent listings.

 ### 10\. Admin Dashboard & Success Metrics

 Administrators can manage users, assets, bookings, payments, reports, and disputes. Key metrics include total rentals, active users, booking completion rate, platform revenue, repeat renters, and customer satisfaction.
